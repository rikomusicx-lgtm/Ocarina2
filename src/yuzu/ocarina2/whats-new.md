## Ocarina2 v1

The first release of Ocarina2: The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, built on zortos293's Lichii fork of Eglantine/Eden — with the Fritchii v5.5-v5.8.4 line merged in full.

- **This build improves performance and stuttering — how the game actually plays, day to day.** Fewer freezes, shorter hitches, better loads, and a steadier time between areas. **It is not intended to improve your FPS.**
- **We know there are still bugs, and we are working on them.** If you hit one, send us your log: Help > Export Log.
- **This is build Ocarina2 v1.**

Not affiliated with Nintendo. Built on Eglantine, Eden, Lichii and Frizti's Fritchii.
